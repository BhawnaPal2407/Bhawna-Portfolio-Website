A bit of background

I'm a final-year Computer Science undergraduate who's spent the last two years moving between two worlds that turn out to be closer than they look: backend engineering and applied machine learning. One semester I'm wiring up REST APIs in Spring Boot; the next, I'm tuning a classification model or watching a multi-agent system make a decision on its own.

During my internship at Ironbook AI, I helped build the INitro Smart Agentic Decisioning System — a multi-agent framework where LLM-driven agents handle autonomous decision-making and task execution using LangChain and LangGraph. Earlier, as a Machine Learning Intern at Smart Swift Innovation, I worked through the full lifecycle of a model: preprocessing, feature engineering, evaluation, and the slow craft of hyperparameter tuning.

Outside of code, I've run live events as a freelance event manager — coordinating vendors, guests, and timelines under real pressure, which turns out to be excellent training for shipping software on a deadline too.
